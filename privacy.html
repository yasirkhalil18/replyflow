Official Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 24, 2026 • Effective Immediately

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to ReplyFlow Automation Suite ("ReplyFlow", "we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and privacy rights. This Privacy Policy governs your use of our website, Discord Bot services, API integrations, and web dashboard applications across all connected platforms.

By connecting your Discord account, authorizing Discord Bot permissions, or using our Web Dashboard, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

To provide automated community management, live telemetry counters, AI support, and audit logging, we collect the following data types:

  • Discord Account Data Discord User ID, Username, Avatar URL, and Guild (Server) Memberships.
  • Server Telemetry Channel names, member counts, voice state timestamps, and server role hierarchies required for Live Server Stats.
  • AI RAG & Chat Memory Non-sensitive chat messages sent in designated AI assistance channels (`#ai-assistant`) or ticket channels to generate automated support responses via Google Gemini AI.
  • Audit Logs & Deletion Archives Temporary text logs of deleted/edited messages, member joins/leaves, and role changes retained strictly for administrator security review in `#audit-logs`.
3. Compliance with Platform Developer Policies

ReplyFlow complies strictly with all third-party developer requirements:

  • Discord Developer Terms: Message content and member data are processed strictly in accordance with Discord's Developer Terms of Service and Policy. Privileged intents (Message Content, Server Members, Presences) are used solely for requested features (AutoMod, Leveling, Audit Logs).
  • Google & YouTube API Services: When social feed updates are enabled, ReplyFlow accesses public channel metadata using YouTube API Services. We do not store or share private Google user data.
  • Meta & Instagram APIs: Social feed alerts only monitor public posts and brand updates authorized by server administrators.
4. How We Protect & Store Your Data

Data security is our top priority. All database records (SQLite / MySQL) are encrypted at rest and transmitted using secure TLS 1.3 protocol. Access to database servers is strictly restricted to automated bot service instances.

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data or chat logs to any third-party advertisers or data brokers under any circumstances.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion Requests

You retain full control over your data. Under GDPR and CCPA guidelines, you have the right to request access to or complete erasure of your stored records (XP levels, ticket transcripts, and audit logs).

To request permanent data deletion, please contact our privacy desk at Contact Desk or email our support team directly. Server administrators may also reset or purge server data at any time via the Web Dashboard.

6. Contact & Legal Enquiries

If you have any questions, concerns, or legal inquiries regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please reach out through our official channels:

• Web Contact: https://replyflow.io/contact.html
• Support Desk: https://replyflow.io/support.html
