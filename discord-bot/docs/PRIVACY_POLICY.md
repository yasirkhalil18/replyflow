# Discord Automation Cloud (SaaS) - Privacy Policy

**Effective Date**: August 5, 2026

Discord Automation Cloud values your privacy. This Privacy Policy details the data we collect, how it is stored, and your rights under GDPR and CCPA.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

- **Discord Account Information**: User ID, username, discriminator, avatar URL, and email address obtained via Discord OAuth2 authorization.
- **Guild Telemetry**: Guild ID, name, icon URL, member count, role names, and channel IDs necessary for bot plugin operation.
- **Message Content**: Temporarily processed in-memory for AutoModeration AI toxicity scans and AI Assistant queries. We do not permanently store message contents unless saved in an active Ticket HTML Transcript.

---

## 2. How We Use Information

We use collected data solely to:
- Render custom Canvas rank cards and welcome banners.
- Calculate Leveling XP rewards and leaderboards.
- Filter spam, raid attacks, and toxicity via AutoMod rules.
- Generate AI assistant replies using RAG knowledge bases.

---

## 3. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

- **Audit Logs & Ticket Transcripts**: Retained for 90 days before automated deletion.
- **Account Deletion**: You may request complete deletion of your user data, XP progress, and server settings by contacting `privacy@your-saas.com` or kicking the bot from your guild.
