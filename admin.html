💡 How to get your 16-character Gmail App Password (3 Easy Steps):
  1. Go to your Google Account Settings (myaccount.google.com/security) → Turn ON 2-Step Verification.
  2. Search for "App Passwords" (or go to myaccount.google.com/apppasswords).
  3. Enter app name e.g. "ReplyFlow" → Click Create → Copy the generated 16-character password and paste it below into SMTP Password!
